Title: CNC Spindle Load & Coolant Flow Operational Manual
Section: Section 4 - Sustained Load Derating & Thermal Expansion

Sustained spindle loading exceeding 90% for more than 4 consecutive production cycles creates high localized thermal friction.
If coolant flow drops below 3.0 L/min or bearing temp rises > 15°C/day, emergency inspection of coolant pump line and heat exchanger is required.
Failure to reduce load risks spindle shaft distortion and permanent motor winding burnout.
