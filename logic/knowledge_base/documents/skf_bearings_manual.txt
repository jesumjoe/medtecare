Title: SKF Rolling Bearings Technical Maintenance Handbook
Section: Chapter 7 - Thermal Degradation & Spindle Bearing Wear

When main spindle bearing operating temperature exceeds 85°C, thermal degradation accelerates exponentially. 
Operating temperatures above 100°C indicate severe lubricant breakdown and outer raceway flaking.
Vibration RMS threshold: ISO 10816-3 Zone D specifies RMS velocity > 4.5 mm/s as critical bearing damage.
Immediate Action: Derate spindle speed by 40%. Perform main bearing replacement (SKF 7208 BECBP) within 24 hours.
