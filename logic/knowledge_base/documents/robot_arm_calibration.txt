Title: Robotic Welding & Packaging Arm Calibration Guide
Section: Section 3 - Joint Torque Drift & Servo Motor Recalibration

Arm position error exceeding 1.0 mm tolerance or joint torque drift indicates harmonic drive gear backlash or encoder misalignment.
At high cycle counts (>450,000 cycles), axis 3 and axis 4 servo motors require full mechanical recalibration and zero-point alignment.
Inspection frequency: Every 50,000 cycles or immediately upon position error alert.
