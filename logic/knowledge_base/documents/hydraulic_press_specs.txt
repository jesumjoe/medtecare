Title: Industrial Hydraulic Press B-12 Service Guide
Section: Section 12 - Viscosity Degradation & Cylinder Overpressure

Standard hydraulic oil ISO VG 46 viscosity must remain between 32 cSt and 68 cSt.
Oil viscosity below 30 cSt leads to pump cavitation, internal seal leakage, and cylinder pressure spikes exceeding 3000 PSI.
Required Maintenance: Immediate oil replacement, fluid flushing, and seal kit replacement (Part #HP-7740).
